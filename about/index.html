About CostCruncher

CostCruncher builds free cost calculators for common home improvement and moving projects. Every estimate is broken into line items — materials, labor, delivery and extras — with the pricing assumptions published on each page.

How we make money

The site is free. When a reader buys tools or materials through our affiliate links, we may earn a small commission from the retailer at no extra cost to the reader. We never sell your data, show pop-ups, or accept payment for favorable coverage.

How we calculate

Each calculator uses national average contractor pricing, published material costs and standard trade formulas (waste factors, coverage rates, pitch multipliers). The full method is shown on every calculator page.
