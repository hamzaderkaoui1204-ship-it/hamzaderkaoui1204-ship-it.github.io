How this estimate works

Every spring the same question: how much mulch do I actually need? Measure wrong and you are either making a second trip or staring at a lonely pile of leftovers. Bulk mulch at $40–$55 per cubic yard is half the price of bags — but only if you need a yard or more.

Enter your bed dimensions and depth below. The calculator converts to cubic yards, prices bulk vs bagged, and adds optional spreading labor so you can compare doing it yourself against hiring it out.

How we calculate

Cubic yards = length × width × depth(in) ÷ 12 ÷ 27. Bulk prices per yard delivered: shredded bark $40, dyed mulch $48, cedar $55, playground chips $80. Bagged equivalent: 13.5 bags per yard at ~$5 each. Spreading labor runs $0.35/sq ft.

Questions people ask

How many cubic yards of mulch do I need?＋

Multiply bed area by depth in inches, divide by 324. A 300 sq ft bed at 2 inches needs 1.85 cubic yards — order 2 and have a little extra.

How many bags of mulch equal a yard?＋

A standard 2-cubic-foot bag holds 0.074 yards, so 13.5 bags ≈ 1 cubic yard. Bulk delivery is roughly half the price once you need more than a yard.

How much does mulch delivery cost?＋

Most suppliers charge a flat $50–$100 delivery fee, often waived above 5–10 yards. Deliveries are dumped in one spot — wheelbarrowing is on you.

Is dyed mulch safe?＋

Modern dyed mulch uses carbon-based or iron-oxide dyes considered safe for plants and pets. Avoid cheap mulches from demolition wood, which can contain arsenic.

How deep should mulch be?＋

2–3 inches for established beds, up to 4 inches for new weed suppression. More than 4 inches suffocates roots and encourages rodents.
