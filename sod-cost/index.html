How this estimate works

Sod turns dirt into a lawn in one day, and it costs $0.55–$1.60 per square foot installed depending on grass variety and site prep. Seed is 10× cheaper but takes a year to look like a lawn. Whether sod is worth it depends on your numbers — this calculator gives you the real ones.

How we calculate

Sod is sold by the pallet (~450 sq ft). Material prices per square foot: fescue $0.35, bermuda $0.40, bluegrass $0.45, St. Augustine $0.60, plus flat delivery. Soil prep runs $0.35/sq ft, old lawn removal $0.45/sq ft, and installation labor $0.55/sq ft.

Questions people ask

How much does a pallet of sod cost?＋

A pallet covers ~450 sq ft and costs $150–$270 for the grass itself, or $320–$500 delivered and installed. Most homes need 5–50 pallets.

How much sod do I need?＋

Measure length × width of the area and add 5% for trimming waste. Divide by 450 to get pallets. A 40×30 yard needs 1,200 sq ft ≈ 3 pallets.

Is sod or seed cheaper?＋

Seed costs $0.05–$0.15 per sq ft vs $0.55–$1.60 for installed sod. Sod wins if you need erosion control, have kids/pets, or want a usable lawn this season.

When is the best time to lay sod?＋

Cool-season grasses (fescue, bluegrass): fall or spring. Warm-season (bermuda, St. Augustine): late spring to summer. Avoid freezing ground and heat waves.

How long until I can walk on new sod?＋

Light use after 2–3 weeks, full use after 4–6 weeks when roots have knitted to the soil. Mow for the first time at 3–4 weeks, never removing more than a third of the blade.
