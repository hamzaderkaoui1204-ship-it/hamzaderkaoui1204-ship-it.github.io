How this estimate works

Drywall pricing is all about the finish level, and almost nobody quotes it precisely. Hanging and taping to a paint-ready Level 4 runs $1.90–$2.60 per square foot; a glass-smooth Level 5 adds more; texture changes the number again. This calculator prices each stage separately so quotes cannot hide anything.

How we calculate

Wall and ceiling area drives everything. Standard 4×8 sheets cover 32 sq ft at ~$16.50 each; fasteners, tape and joint compound add $0.55/sq ft. Hanging labor runs $1.10/sq ft. Finishing adds $0.60/sq ft for Level 4 (paint-ready), $1.00 for Level 5 (glass smooth), or $0.55–$0.65 for orange peel/knockdown texture.

Questions people ask

How much does it cost to drywall a 12×12 room?

Hanging and finishing the walls of a 12×12 room with 8-ft ceilings costs $750–$1,200. Adding the ceiling brings it to $1,100–$1,700.

What is a Level 5 drywall finish?

The highest finish: a skim coat over the entire surface for a glass-smooth wall. It is required for glossy paint or direct lighting and adds 30–50% over standard Level 4.

How thick is drywall and which do I need?

Half-inch for walls and ceilings (code standard in most areas), 5/8-inch for fire-rated walls, garage ceilings and between floors. 5/8-inch costs ~40% more per sheet.

How long does drywall take to dry between coats?

Joint compound needs 24 hours between coats — a full 3-coat finish is a 4–7 day process including drying. Texture is applied after the final coat dries.

Can I drywall over existing drywall?

Technically yes over sound walls, but you lose 1/2 inch at every outlet box and door jamb. Removing old drywall usually ends up cheaper and lets you inspect wiring and add insulation.
