How this estimate works

Flooring quotes are quoted per square foot installed, but the sticker rate hides removal, underlayment and waste. Luxury vinyl plank at $7–$16 installed and laminate at $6–$12 look similar in a showroom but differ in lifespan and resale value. Run your room through this calculator to see the real totals.

Questions people ask

What is the cheapest flooring to install?

Carpet at $4–$9 per square foot installed, followed closely by laminate at $6–$12. LVP is the best value if water resistance matters — kitchens, baths, basements.

How much flooring do I need for a 200 sq ft room?

Buy 220 sq ft — 10% extra for cutting waste (15% for diagonal layouts). Keep leftovers: manufacturers discontinue patterns constantly.

Can I install LVP myself?

Click-lock LVP is the most DIY-friendly hard flooring — a weekend project for a bedroom with basic tools. Save the labor line ($4/sq ft) if you are patient with layouts.

Does hardwood flooring add home value?

Yes — hardwood is a top buyer preference and typically recoups 70–100% of cost at resale. Refinishing existing wood ($3–$8/sq ft) beats replacing it.

How long does flooring installation take?

A pro lays 300–500 sq ft of LVP or laminate per day, hardwood 200–300 sq ft. Removal adds a day. A whole single-story home is typically a 3–5 day job.
