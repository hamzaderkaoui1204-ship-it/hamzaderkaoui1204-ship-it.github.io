How this estimate works

Fence quotes are driven by one number most homeowners never see: cost per linear foot, material and labor combined. Privacy wood runs $17–$31 per foot installed, vinyl $21–$42, and aluminum $32–$54. Gates, slopes and old-fence removal change the math again. This calculator breaks all of it out.

How we calculate

Posts are spaced every 8 feet (posts = sections + 1) at ~$38 each including concrete. Panel material is priced per linear foot by style and height: wood privacy $9/ft material + $12/ft labor, chain link $7 + $7, vinyl $16 + $15, aluminum $27 + $22, 3-rail farm $8 + $10. Each 4-foot gate adds ~$275 including hardware. Old fence removal is estimated at $3/ft.

Questions people ask

How much does a 150-foot privacy fence cost?

A 150-foot wood privacy fence at 6 feet tall typically costs $2,600–$4,600 installed, including one gate. Vinyl would run $3,400–$6,200 for the same footprint.

What is the cheapest fence to install?

Chain link at $12–$19 per linear foot, followed by farm-style rail fencing. If you need privacy on a budget, wood privacy is the cheapest solid option.

How deep do fence posts need to be?

Posts should be buried at least 1/3 of the above-ground height — 24 inches for a 6-foot fence — and set in concrete. This is where cheap contractors cut corners.

How long does a wood fence last?

Pressure-treated pine lasts 15–20 years; cedar 20–30. Vinyl and aluminum last 30–50 years with essentially zero maintenance.

Do I need a permit for a fence?

Most cities require a permit for fences over 6 feet, and some require one for any fence. Budget $50–$200 and check setback rules before you build.
