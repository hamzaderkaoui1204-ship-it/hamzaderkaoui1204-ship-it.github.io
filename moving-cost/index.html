How this estimate works

Moving quotes are famously opaque: hourly rates for local moves, weight-and-distance pricing for long distance, and a dozen add-ons in the fine print. National averages run $800–$2,500 for a local move and $2,500–$11,000 cross-country. This calculator prices both models transparently.

Enter your home size, crew size and distance. You will see the pricing model the movers will actually use, broken into labor, truck, travel, and optional packing and stairs charges at 2026 rates.

What's included

  • Mover labor (load, transport, unload)
  • Truck and fuel
  • Optional full packing service
  • Stairs surcharge

Not included

  • Packing materials (boxes, tape) — add $100–$400
  • Insurance beyond released value ($0.60/lb)
  • Specialty items (pianos, safes, hot tubs)
  • Storage ($50–$200/month)

How we calculate

Local moves (under 50 miles) are priced hourly: 2 movers at ~$60/hr ($30/mover), 3 at $90/hr, with typical crew hours by home size. Long-distance moves use a size-based base charge plus $1.15/mile plus loading labor. Packing service adds $150–$800 by home size; each flight of stairs adds $75.

Questions people ask

How much do movers cost per hour?

Local movers charge $25–$35 per mover per hour, so a 2-person crew runs $50–$70/hour plus truck and travel fees. Most homes need 4–8 crew hours.

How much should I tip movers?

Standard tip is 10–15% of the move cost, or $20–$40 per mover for half-day jobs. Tip in cash at the end, split between the crew.

How far in advance should I book movers?

Book 4–6 weeks out for summer and month-end moves, 2 weeks is usually enough mid-winter. Peak dates (June–August, first of month) price 20–30% higher.

Is it cheaper to rent a truck and move myself?

DIY with a rental truck costs $50–$300 local and $1,000–$2,500 cross country — roughly half a full-service move. Container companies (PODS, U-Pack) land in between.

What can movers not transport?

Flammables, propane, gasoline, plants on long moves, perishable food and hazardous chemicals. Valuables like jewelry and documents should move with you personally.
