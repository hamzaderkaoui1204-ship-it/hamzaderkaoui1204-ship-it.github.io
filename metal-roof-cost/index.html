How this estimate works

Metal roofing costs 2–3× asphalt shingles, but it lasts 40–70 years and can cut insurance and cooling costs. The catch: quotes range from $5 per square foot for corrugated panels to $16+ for standing seam, and roof pitch quietly inflates the actual area you are paying for. This calculator handles all of it.

How we calculate

Actual roof area = footprint × pitch factor (low 1.05, medium 1.15, steep 1.30). Installed pricing per square foot: corrugated panels $3.50 material + $2.75 labor, stone-coated metal shingle $5.50 + $4.00, standing seam $7.50 + $6.00. Underlayment and ice-and-water shield add $0.75/sq ft; trim and flashing $1.10/sq ft; tear-off of an existing shingle roof $1.15/sq ft.

Questions people ask

How much does a metal roof cost for a 1,800 sq ft home?＋

Expect $9,500–$15,300 for corrugated panels, $13,700–$22,100 for stone-coated shingles, and $18,500–$29,700 for standing seam, installed with tear-off.

Is a metal roof cheaper than shingles over time?＋

Asphalt shingles cost $4–$6/sq ft but last 15–25 years. Metal costs 2–3× upfront but lasts 40–70 years — over 50 years, metal is usually cheaper even ignoring energy savings.

Does a metal roof lower insurance?＋

In hail- and wind-prone states, many insurers offer 5–35% discounts for impact-resistant metal roofing. In wildfire zones, some carriers require it.

How noisy is a metal roof?＋

With solid decking and underlayment, a metal roof is only marginally louder than shingles. The "rain on a tin roof" sound comes from metal installed over open framing.

Can metal panels go over existing shingles?＋

Often yes — one shingle layer can stay in place, saving $1.15+/sq ft in tear-off. Check local code and manufacturer warranty terms first.
