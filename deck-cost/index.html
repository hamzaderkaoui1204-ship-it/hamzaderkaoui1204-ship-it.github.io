How this estimate works

A deck is one of the best-value outdoor projects — and one of the easiest to overpay for. The material choice alone swings the price 2×: pressure-treated wood decking runs $15–$32 per square foot installed while composite runs $24–$52. Height, railing and stairs add more. This calculator itemizes every component.

Questions people ask

How much does a 12×16 deck cost?

A 12×16 pressure-treated wood deck costs $3,000–$5,600 installed. The same deck in composite runs $4,800–$8,200. Railing, stairs and elevation add to both figures.

Is a composite deck worth the extra cost?

Composite costs 50–80% more upfront but needs no staining and lasts 25–30 years vs 15–20 for wood. If you plan to stay 10+ years, the lower maintenance usually wins.

How much does a DIY deck cost?

Materials only: $8–$14 per square foot for wood, $14–$20 for composite. You save roughly 40–50% vs hiring out, but mistakes in framing are expensive to fix.

Do decks add home value?

A well-built deck recoups roughly 50–80% of its cost in resale value, and outdoor living space is a top ask from buyers. Wood decks historically recoup slightly more than composite.

Do I need a permit to build a deck?

Most jurisdictions require a permit for any attached deck or platform above 30 inches. Budget $150–$500 and expect footing inspections on elevated decks.
